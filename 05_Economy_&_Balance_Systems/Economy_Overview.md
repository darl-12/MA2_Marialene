# Progression & Resources

**Tuklas-Eh!** uses a single currency, **points**, which the player earns by winning Coffee Making rounds, with bonus points from win streaks and achievements, and spends only on customizing their café. Points are never lost when a round is lost, so a loss costs the player nothing. There is no secondary currency or crafting system. Other rewards are collected instead of spent: a [[Puzzle Pieces|puzzle piece]] comes every 5 rounds and unlocks a story that restores part of [[Tuklas]]’ memory, and badges come from achievements. [[Tuklas]] has no stats or upgrades, so progression shows up in the [[Cultural Awareness Rate]] (CAR), which rises toward 75% as the game moves from degraded to restored, and in the five [[puzzle pieces]]. 

- **Primary Currency**: Points. Earned every winning round of the Coffee Making Mini-Game, plus bonuses from win streaks and achievements. Spent on café customization. Points are never lost when a round is lost.
- **Secondary Currency**: None. Milestone rewards are not spendable: [[puzzle pieces]] unlock stories, and badges are collected from achievements.
- **Crafting Materials & Components**: Not used in this game.
