
# World Setting

**Setting**: The game takes place in a 2D, hand-drawn version of Lipa City, Batangas, inspired by its coffee heritage and local culture. Players explore the city's coffee-making process through the Coffee Making mini-game while learning about the history of [[kapeng barako]] through fun facts and story elements. As players progress, their actions influence Lipa's [[Cultural Awareness Rate ]](CAR), which reflects the city's cultural condition. The environment changes depending on the CAR:

- **Degraded**: The surroundings appear dull and less lively, reflecting the lack of cultural awareness.
- **Maintained**: The environment becomes more vibrant, with warmer colors and signs of improvement.
- **Restored**: The city becomes colorful and lively, showing the appreciation and preservation of Lipa's coffee heritage.

The game has a nostalgic and curious atmosphere, allowing players to explore Lipa's coffee heritage and learn more about its history. As players progress, the environment becomes warmer, brighter, and more lively, creating a hopeful and encouraging feeling as the city is gradually restored.

**Central Conflict:** Lipa City's coffee heritage is slowly fading as fewer young people become familiar with its history and traditions. At the same time, [[Tuklas]] is trying to recover his lost memories and discover the purpose behind his creation. As players complete coffee orders, they help increase the [[Cultural Awareness Rate]] (CAR), restore the city, and unlock stories about Lipa's coffee heritage. Through the collection of [[puzzle pieces]], players gradually uncover [[Tuklas]]' memories and work toward answering the question, "Why was I made?" The main goal is to prepare coffee orders accurately, collect puzzle pieces, and restore both Lipa's cultural awareness and [[Tuklas]]'s memories.

**Themes:**
1. **Cultural Heritage Preservation** – Highlighting the importance of preserving Lipa City's coffee heritage, particularly kapeng barako.
2. **Community and Tradition** – Recognizing the role of local farmers and the community in keeping Lipa's coffee traditions alive.
3. **Identity and Self-Discovery** – Follows [[Tuklas]]' journey of recovering his memories and understanding his purpose and connection to the world. 

# Characters

**Protagonist (Player):** [[Tuklas]]
- **Background**: A curious robot who wears a barong-inspired outfit. After a thunderstorm short-circuits him and wipes his memories, he wakes up in an unfamiliar place with no idea who he is or why he was made.
- **Motivation**: Find his creator and learn why he was made.
- **Gameplay function**: The player controls [[Tuklas]] through the interactive map and the tap-timing coffee-order mini-game. Successful rounds raise the CAR, failed rounds lower it, and every five rounds earn a [[Puzzle Pieces|puzzle piece]] that unlocks a character story and recover his memory.

**Antagonist:** 
- **Motivation**: The antagonist is not a person but the gradual loss of cultural awareness as people begin to forget Lipa City's coffee heritage and traditions. It represents the lack of interest in learning about and preserving the city's history. The degraded appearance of the game serves as its visual representation, showing how cultural heritage slowly fades when it is neglected.
- **Gameplay presence:** The antagonist is reflected through the [[Cultural Awareness Rate]] (CAR) and the changing environment. When players make mistakes, the [[Cultural Awareness Rate|CAR]] decreases, and the surroundings become more degraded. As players complete coffee orders and increase the [[Cultural Awareness Rate|CAR]], the environment gradually becomes more vibrant and restored.

**Key NPC (Guide): A Veteran Coffee Farmer**
- **Motivation**: To share their knowledge and experiences about Lipa's coffee heritage with the younger generation and ensure that these traditions are passed on to others.
- **Gameplay Function:** Serves as one of the key characters who shares stories about Lipa's coffee heritage through the [[puzzle pieces]] unlocked by players. Their stories will be developed with the help of local coffee farmers and Museo ng Lipa historians to ensure the accuracy of the information presented in the game.
