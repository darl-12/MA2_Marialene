

# World Setting

Setting: The game takes place in a 2D, hand-drawn, storybook version of Lipa City, Batangas, present-day but touched by the long shadow of the 1880s coffee rust and the industry’s decline. The player moves along an interactive map of the kapeng barako process, from farm to cup. The visual tone shifts with the Cultural Awareness Rate (CAR):

- Degraded: desaturated, rusted, overgrown, silent.
    
- Maintained: muted but stable, with warm light.
    
- Restored: golden, lively, full of color and ambient life such as roosters, chatter and roasting sounds.
    

The mood is nostalgic, curious and quietly melancholic, becoming warm and hopeful as the world is restored.

Central Conflict: Lipa’s coffee heritage is fading from living memory. Farmers have no successors, traditions go undocumented, and young people no longer meet this history. This mirrors the protagonist’s own condition, since a storm erased Tuklas’s memories and left him a stranger to the world he was built for. The player fights this fading by fulfilling coffee orders, which raises the CAR, restores the land, and recovers lost stories and memories. In the story, the question is “Why was I made?” In gameplay, the objective is to prepare orders accurately, collect puzzle pieces, and bring the world and Tuklas’s memory back from decay.

Themes:

1. Memory and forgetting: the cost of letting heritage, or oneself, fade.
    
2. Identity and belonging: discovering who you are through your roots, as kapeng barako is tied to Batangueño identity.
    
3. Passing the torch: knowledge and purpose are meant to be handed down.
    

# Characters

Protagonist (Player): Tuklas

- Background: A curious robot with a poet’s heart who once stood in the rain just to marvel at the world. A thunderstorm short-circuited him and wiped his memories. He wakes in an unfamiliar world holding only a mysterious symbol.
    
- Motivation: Find his creator and learn why he was made.
    
- Gameplay function: The player controls Tuklas through the interactive map and the tap-timing coffee-order mini-game. Successful rounds raise the CAR, failed rounds lower it, and every five rounds earn a puzzle piece that unlocks a character story and restores part of the symbol and his memory.
    

  

Antagonist: 

- Motivation: Not a person but a creeping force of neglect and forgetting. It represents the coffee rust that devastated Lipa’s Arabica and the rust that threatens Tuklas’s circuits. It “wins” when heritage is no longer remembered.
    
- Gameplay presence: It appears as the pressure on the CAR. Mistakes spread rust visuals across the map and push the world toward the degraded state, while harder mini-game conditions appear as the CAR changes (longer orders). {Each zone has a boss-like “rust surge” event.?}
