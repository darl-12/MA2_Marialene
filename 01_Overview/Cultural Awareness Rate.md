The **Cultural Awareness Rate (CAR)** is the game's main performance meter, showing how well the player is doing in the coffee-making mini-game and how much of Lipa's coffee heritage has been restored. It starts at 50%, rises with each win, and falls with each loss, though no points are ever lost. CAR also adapts the difficulty of the next round, getting harder after wins and easier after losses, so beginners aren't frustrated and experts aren't bored. It also changes how Lipa looks, shifting the world between degraded, maintained, and restored. The goal is to raise CAR to 75% or higher to fully restore Lipa, and since losing never ends the game, players can always try again and see their effort reflected in the world.
