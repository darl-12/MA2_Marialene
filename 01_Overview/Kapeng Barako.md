Kapeng Barako is Lipa, Batangas’s signature coffee, made from the rust-resistant Coffea liberica that replaced Arabica after the coffee rust outbreak of the late 1880s. It is a symbol of Batangueño identity and the reason Lipa is called the country’s “original coffee capital.” Today, the industry behind it is in decline, with fewer farmers and few successors, and the province is working to document and protect its history and identity.
