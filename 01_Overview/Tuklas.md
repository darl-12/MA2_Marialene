**Tuklas** is a small, curious robot who wears a barong-inspired outfit. He wakes in an unfamiliar place, with no memory of who he is or why he was made. Players guide him through Lipa City, where he prepares and serves coffee orders and learns about the city’s kapeng barako heritage from the people he meets. Each round shows on his expressive face, and every five rounds he earns a puzzle pieces that reveal more of Tuklas's story and help him recover his lost memories. His goal is to find his creator and answer the question “Why was I made?”.
