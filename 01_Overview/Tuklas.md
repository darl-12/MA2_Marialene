**Tuklas** is a small, curious robot who wears a barong-inspired outfit. After a thunderstorm short-circuits him and wipes his memories, he wakes up in an unfamiliar place with no idea who he is or why he was made. Players guide him through Lipa City, where he prepares and serves coffee orders while learning about the city's kapeng barako heritage from the people he meets. Each round is reflected through his expressive face, and every five rounds, he earns a puzzle piece that reveals more of his story and helps him recover his lost memories. His goal is to find his creator and answer the question, "Why was I made?"
