Puzzle pieces are the main milestone reward in **Tuklas-Eh!.** The player earns one for every five rounds of the coffee-making mini-game, and each piece unlocks a short story about a character native to Lipa. There are five in total, one for each stage. Each story restores a part of Tuklas’s memory, so collecting all five answers his question of why he was made. Each story also reflects a part of Lipa’s coffee history and culture, from the coffee rust outbreak and the shift to liberica to the effects of the Taal eruption and the pandemic, the decline in successors, and the effort to preserve[[ kapeng barako]]’s identity. Along the way, the stories introduce players to the people behind Lipa’s coffee heritage, such as farmers, drying-yard workers, roasters, market vendors, and a museum curator.
