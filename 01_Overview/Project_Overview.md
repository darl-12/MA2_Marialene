**Tuklas-Eh!** is a casual 2D mobile game where players help [[Tuklas]], a robot wearing a barong-inspired outfit, explore Lipa City's coffee heritage while uncovering the story behind his creation. The game features a coffee-making mini-game where players tap the correct ingredients at the right time to complete orders and earn points. Players can use their points to customize their café, build win and lose streaks, unlock achievements, and increase Lipa's [[Cultural Awareness Rate ]](CAR). The CAR also affects the game's difficulty and shows the progress of Lipa's cultural restoration. After each round, players discover fun facts about Lipa's coffee heritage, particularly kapeng barako. As players progress, they can collect puzzle pieces that reveal more of Tuklas's story and help him recover his lost memories. The goal is to increase CAR to at least 75% and collect puzzle pieces to complete Tuklas's journey.
# High Concept & Pitch

![[game title.png|458]]

You play as [[Tuklas]], a small, curious robot with a barong-inspired outfit and red scarf, who must prepare and serve coffee orders with accurate tap timing using the [[Cultural Awareness Rate]] (CAR), a performance meter that adapts difficulty and shifts the world between degraded, maintained, and restored to achieve the rediscovery and restoration of Lipa's kapeng barako heritage, one story and fun fact at a time.
## Genre

This game has two primary genres: Casual Game and Educational Game. Its secondary genres are Mini-Game and Cultural-Based Learning, as it combines simple coffee-making gameplay with learning about Lipa City's coffee heritage, particularly kapeng barako. The game is designed for single-player only, with no multiplayer, co-op, or leaderboards, allowing players to enjoy the game at their own pace. It has a warm, playful, and encouraging mood, with [[Tuklas]]’ expressive face and the world’s visible recovery showing the player's progress. Through its simple gameplay, fun facts, and story progression, the game makes learning about local culture more enjoyable and engaging.
## Target Audience

This game primarily targets Filipino youth, especially Lipa City residents, who may have limited knowledge of Kapeng Barako and the city's coffee heritage. It also caters to casual mobile players, students, educators, local tourism offices, coffee farmers, cultural workers, and people from other parts of the Philippines who are interested in learning about Batangueño culture through a simple and enjoyable game.

## Target Platforms

This game is primarily designed for mobile devices, particularly smartphones. Its 2D format keeps the game assets lightweight, allowing it to run on a wide range of phones. Players can install and play the game on their own devices, making it accessible anytime and anywhere. It is designed for short gaming sessions, whether at home, during commutes or breaks, and even in classroom and community settings.
