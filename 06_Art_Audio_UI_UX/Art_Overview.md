# Art & Visual Style

##### Visual Style
The game features a 2D hand-drawn aesthetic designed for fixed portrait orientation on mobile devices. The visual tone balances cultural heritage with modern casual gaming, portraying Lipa City’s coffee history in a warm, welcoming, and playful manner.

##### Color Palette

- **Background**: mint, teal gray, sky blue, meadow green, light wood, dark wood, gray
- **Foreground**: black for outlines and text, white and red for [[Tuklas]] and his scarf, navy blue for his face, deep teal for text boxes, golden yellow, light yellow, and cream for the logo and title text
- **Interactive elements**: orange for button (pause and restart), brown for button rims and icons, tan for lighter accents
- **Feedback**: meadow green when the [[Cultural Awareness Rate|CAR]] rises, rust orange when it falls
- **World states**: degraded is gray, restored is golden yellow with warm orange

# User Interface (HUD)

The main Coffee Making mini-game interface uses a landscape-oriented, touch-friendly HUD designed for easy gameplay on mobile devices.

- **Customer Order Display**: A panel at the top of the screen showing the required coffee ingredients and timing targets.
- **CAR Meter**: A progress bar displaying the current [[Cultural Awareness Rate]] (CAR) percentage and the café's restoration progress.
- **Tuklas Reaction Panel:** A character display showing [[Tuklas]]'s reactions to the player's timing and streaks.
- **Ingredient Action Panel**: Large, touch-friendly ingredient buttons for easy selection during gameplay.
- **Pause Icon:** A button in the top corner that opens the pause menu during gameplay.
